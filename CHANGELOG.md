# What changed

Newest first. Each entry names the **build id** it first shipped in, which is the
short code in the site's footer (`build 74g3`). If your build id is older than
the entry, you have not got that change yet: hard-refresh the page.

Bugs and wrong numbers go in [Issues](https://github.com/Jadeon/HeroBuilder-IssueTracker/issues).

## build 74g3 — 19 August 2026

**Sign in.** You can now sign in with Google or Steam. It does nothing yet
beyond signing you in: saved builds are the next thing being built. The site
asks Google for an identifier and nothing else, so it never receives your name,
your email address or your picture.

**Crafted charms stay on the grid.** Crafting a charm, adding an affix and then
closing the editor put nothing on the board. The charm was in your build and
invisible. If you tried to craft a charm before this build and it vanished, that
was this, and it is fixed.

**Jewel sockets on the Incarnation board.** The eight socket nodes accept
socketables now. A jewel only counts once you have actually allocated the node it
sits in.

**Search every item without picking a category first.** The items page listed
categories and made you choose one before you could search. All 1,119 items are
now searchable from one place.

**Flat elemental damage is added up for you.** The game shows six separate flat
elemental stats and never totals them. The Build tab now does. It counts only the
elemental stats, not the relic damage the game adds into the same figure.

**The item count was wrong.** The items page claimed 1,141 items over a list that
added up to 1,119. Some were being counted twice. It says 1,119 now.

**Tier ranges read "D to S"** instead of joining the letters with a dash.
